# Fill the six W's

The `human_context` block carries the points a scanner can't infer:

- **who** — who is this for
- **what** — what are they building
- **why** — why does it exist
- **where** — where does it run
- **when** — when it started / the timeline
- **how** — how it's built

Open `project.faf`, fill each key with one honest line, and save. The sidebar
marks every slot ● populated or ○ empty as you go. Empty slots are one click
away in the **FAF Context** view.
